SUMMARY (not bill text) of H.B. 76 (2026), Data Center Water Transparency Amendments, as presented by the
Division of Water Rights to the Natural Resources, Agriculture, and Environment Interim Committee (Aug 19, 2026):

- Large data centers must submit projected water use, discharge, treatment, and water reuse plans before construction.
- Data centers using 75 acre-feet of water or more annually must report water use and conservation efforts each year.
- Water use information is published online to support transparency and public awareness.
- The Division of Water Rights is responsible for collecting reports and enforcement.

Expected lever: reporting_threshold_af = 75 (changes what the state knows, not water use).
Source: https://le.utah.gov/interim/2026/pdf/00003109.pdf
