Advanced: how risk is scored

Risk combines four factors, scaled 0 to 1 and weighted: demand compared with remaining supply (capped at 1.5x), share of junior water rights, thin storage (storage as a share of annual use), and municipal and industrial use growth (capped at 25 percent per five years). The weights, caps and category cut-offs (Low under 40, Watch 40 to 55, High 55 to 70, Critical 70 and above) are my own simplifying choices and are not an official method. Adjust the weights below or replace the method.
