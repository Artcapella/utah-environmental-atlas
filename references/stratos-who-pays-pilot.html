Stratos: who gets the benefits and who pays

A pilot for one project. It lists benefits and costs by who experiences them, shows ranges with their sources, and says plainly where nothing is published. It does not produce a net score.

Read before using. Figures were gathered from news coverage, advocacy reports, government statements and developer claims in 2026, and have not been independently verified. Most environmental figures are modeled estimates, some disputed by the developer, and none are peer-reviewed as far as I could find. Items marked "my arithmetic" are my own conversions, not published numbers. Project size and power plans are still changing, so sources conflict. This is a framework for discussion, not a basis for a decision.

Water test from Executive Order 2026-03

The governor's office describes one principle of the order as ensuring water consumption is not increased. This test compares the project's estimated net water use with what the land uses today. I wrote the test myself to make the principle concrete. How the order is actually applied is up to the agencies.

Source types

Each figure carries a tag with a distinct outline, so it does not depend on color. Government: statements or projections from officials. Developer: claims from the project's backers. Independent academic: a university researcher. Advocacy-commissioned: produced or funded by an advocacy group. My arithmetic: a conversion or division I did.
