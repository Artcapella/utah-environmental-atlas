How the score works. Each factor is scaled 0 to 1, then combined with the weights above and shown as 0 to 100. Demand ratio = pending demand / (headroom x dry-year factor), capped at 1.5. Junior share is the fraction of rights that are junior. Storage buffer risk = 1 minus storage as a fraction of annual use (capped at 100 percent). M&I trend is capped at 25 percent growth per five years. These caps and the scaling are my own simplifying choices, not an official method, so validate or change them.
