Advanced: how risk is scored

Risk combines four factors, scaled 0 to 1 and weighted: counted project demand compared with remaining supply (capped at 1.5x), share of junior water rights, thin storage, and municipal and industrial use growth (capped at 25 percent per five years). Weights, caps and category cut-offs (Low under 40, Watch 40 to 55, High 55 to 70, Critical 70 and above) are my own simplifying choices, not an official method. Projects are counted in full from their start year, so phasing is not modeled.

What is known and not known

From your research notes: under HB 76 (effective July 1, 2026), new data centers over 10,000 sq ft that will draw 75 af or more a year must report to the Division of Water Rights. No reports had been published yet, so the first actual-use numbers should appear around September 2027. The notes also say no public source projects a statewide data center water total over the next five years, and that Stratos has changed its filings several times. Developer figures often cover only cooling inside the data halls, not water for on-site gas power plants.
