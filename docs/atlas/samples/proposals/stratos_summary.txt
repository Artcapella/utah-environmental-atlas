SUMMARY FOR EXTRACTION TESTING, compiled from 2026 news coverage (not a developer document).

The Stratos Project in Box Elder County is planned at up to 9 gigawatts of power at full buildout, with on-site natural gas generation for early phases. A developer engineering report projects about 1,215 acre-feet per year of water diverted at full buildout and about 987 acre-feet per year of net depletion after return flows. The developer plans to purchase about 3,000 acre-feet of water rights and has about 10,000 acre-feet under contract. The project footprint was reported at about 40,000 acres and later about 20,000 acres.
