H.B. 9999 Data Center Cooling Standards (mock)

Section 1. A large data center that generates electricity on site using natural gas shall use dry or air-cooled condensers for any steam cycle, and may not use wet-recirculating cooling towers for power generation.

Section 2. A large data center shall use closed-loop cooling for its data halls, with no continuous evaporative consumption after initial fill.

Section 3. The Office of Energy Development shall publish an annual summary of large data center electricity generation.
