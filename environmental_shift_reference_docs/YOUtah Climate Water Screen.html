            Terms, method and sources

            Acre-foot (AF)
            About 326,000 gallons. A typical Utah household uses roughly half an acre-foot a year indoors.
            Cooling (direct) water
            Water used at the data center, mostly evaporated to remove heat from servers.
            Power-plant (indirect) water
            Water consumed to generate the electricity the data center uses. Federal researchers estimate it is about 12 times direct use nationally.
            Consumptive use
            Water that evaporates or is otherwise lost and does not return to a river, aquifer or the lake. This is what matters most downstream.
            WUE
            Water-use effectiveness: liters of water per kilowatt-hour of server energy. Lower is better.
            PUE
            Power usage effectiveness: total facility energy divided by server energy. Lower is better.
            Blowdown
            Salty water flushed from evaporative cooling systems, discharged under a water-quality permit.
            How the estimate is calculated

            Annual facility energy (MWh) = capacity × 8,760 hours × utilization. Server energy = facility energy ÷ PUE. Cooling water = server energy × WUE. Power-plant water = facility energy × water consumed per MWh generated. Ranges apply ±15 points of utilization with the low and high value for each option.
